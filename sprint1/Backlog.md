## User stories
| # | Story | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | As a producer, I want to submit product details so customers can buy them | High |
| 2 | As a producer, I want to be told what is invalid so I can correct it | High |
| 3 | As a producer, I want my organic claim verified so customers can trust it | High |
| 4 | As a producer, I want to update stock and availability dates | High |
| 5 | As the platform, I want zero-stock products hidden automatically | High |
| 6 | As a customer, I want to add products from several producers to one basket | High |
| 7 | As a customer, I want unavailable items flagged at checkout | High |
| 8 | As a customer, I want to retry if payment is declined | Medium |
| 9 | As the platform, I want 5% commission calculated per producer | Medium |
| 10 | As an admin, I want to trace affected customers during a food incident | Medium |

## Sprint plan
- **Sprint 1 (done solo):** strategic BPMN, analysis, scope, backlog, repository.
- **Sprint 2:** i* SD and SR models, two operational BPMN models, first Camunda
  processes, forms and service tasks.
- **Sprint 3:** full execution, integration, exception handling, legal, ethical,
  social and professional evaluation, demo.
